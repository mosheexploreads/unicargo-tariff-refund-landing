Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Document No. TBK-PRIV-2026-US

TariffBack is operated by Unicargo LTD. ("TariffBack," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when using our IEEPA tariff refund analysis services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you submit our audit request form, we collect:

  • Company name and registered address
  • Work email address and contact name
  • EIN / IRS tax identification number
  • Annual tariff spend range
  • ACE registration status

Entry data you provide for analysis

To perform our analysis, we ask you to provide ACE export reports (ES-001 Entry Summary and ES-003 Entry Summary Line Item reports). These reports contain customs entry data including HTS classifications, country of origin, declared valuations, and duty amounts.

Payment information

If you submit a filing fee, payment card details are processed by our payment processor and are not stored on our servers. We retain only the transaction amount and reference for escrow administration purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • Performing the IEEPA tariff refund eligibility analysis you requested
  • Preparing a CAPE Declaration CSV file for your review and self-submission
  • Communicating with you about your audit and next steps
  • Administering the BDO escrow arrangement for your filing fee
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to your engagement with us.

3. Confidentiality

TariffBack treats all entry data, financial information, and business information you provide as strictly confidential. Your data is used solely for the purpose of performing the analysis described in our service agreement. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal or company data with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • BDO Escrow Administration: Transaction details necessary to administer the escrow arrangement are shared with BDO as escrow agent.
  • Service providers: We use limited third-party tools (email, cloud storage) to operate our service. These providers are contractually bound to confidentiality and may not use your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority.

We explicitly do not submit any of your data to CBP or any government agency. You, as Importer of Record, are solely responsible for any submissions made to CBP.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to complete your engagement and for a period of 7 years thereafter to comply with applicable record-keeping obligations. Entry data provided for analysis is deleted from our active systems upon completion of your engagement.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. All data is transmitted over encrypted (SSL/TLS) connections. Access to client data is restricted to team members directly involved in your engagement.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the address below. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use basic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect anonymous usage data and do not identify you personally. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
