Frequently Asked Questions

How much will this cost?

+
The audit is completely free. If you decide to file with us, our fee is just 5% of the refund we actually recover for you — and even that is held in escrow by BDO and released only once your CBP refund arrives. You keep 95%, and you risk nothing.

What is the escrow process?

+
After your free audit, if you choose to file, our 5% fee is committed to a BDO escrow account (not paid to Unicargo). BDO holds it until your CBP refund is processed. Once your refund arrives, BDO releases the 5% to us and you keep the rest. We're never paid before you are.

Can I get my money back from escrow if I change my mind?

+
Yes. The audit is free and carries no obligation. The 5% fee is only committed to escrow if you decide to file with us, and it's released to us solely when your refund arrives. See our service agreement for full details.

Do you need my actual tariff data?

+
We need read-only access to your ACE account. You control the access via the CBP system — you can revoke it anytime. We never download or store your data on our servers.

What if I'm not ACE registered yet?

+
We help. Most companies complete ACE registration in 1–2 weeks with our guidance. Or, if you prefer, we can give you a quick preliminary estimate first — free and with no obligation.

What's the difference between Phase 1 and Phase 2?

+
Phase 1 (80 days from entry liquidation): File via CBP CAPE portal — straightforward, 60–90 day refund processing. Phase 2 (after 80 days): Requires formal CBP protest — complex, longer timeline. Our audit tells you exactly which path applies to each entry.

How long does filing take?

+
The CAPE filing itself takes 1–2 weeks. CBP then processes refunds in 60–90 days. Phase 2 protests take longer (depends on CBP workload).

Will BDO escrow charge me fees?

+
No. There's nothing extra to pay — the audit is free and our only charge is the 5% filing fee, which already covers the BDO escrow arrangement.

What if you find $0 refund?

+
Then you pay nothing. The audit is free, and our 5% fee only ever applies to money we actually recover. No refund, no fee.

Can I see a sample audit before committing?

+
Yes. We provide a real, anonymized audit sample on this page. Every audit follows the same format — 100% reconciled, entry-by-entry breakdown, Phase 1 vs Phase 2 split.
